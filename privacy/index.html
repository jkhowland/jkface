JK Bold Face

Privacy Policy — effective November 6, 2026

What this app does

JK Bold Face is a Garmin watch face. If you choose to connect Google Calendar, it shows the title and start time of your next event for the current day on the watch.

Data accessed

With your consent, the app requests read-only access to your Google Calendar (scope calendar.readonly). It reads only the title and start time of events on your primary calendar for the current day.

Where data goes

  • Requests go directly from your watch to Google. There is no server operated by the developer.
  • Your Google sign-in tokens and the next event's title and time are stored only on your watch.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to any third party.

Revoking access

Remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or uninstall the watch face to delete all stored data.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
